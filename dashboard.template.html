      How it works

      1. Runs on its own. Every store is checked every few minutes while your PC is on and you’re signed in.
      2. Only real stock counts. Pre-orders and reservations show as out of stock. “Unconfirmed” means the store says in stock but it couldn’t be verified — call first.
      3. You’re told straight away. A Windows alert pops up; for priority stores you also get a phone call and the store page opens.
      4. You pay. Ordering needs your login and the card’s SMS code, so the last step is yours.
      Prices and stock come straight from each store’s website. This page updates itself every 30 seconds.
